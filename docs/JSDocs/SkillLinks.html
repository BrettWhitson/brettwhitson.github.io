SkillLinks - Connects each skill to the roles where it was used

Hovering or focusing a skill chip highlights every chip for that skill and the roles that list it, and dims the rest; tapping or pressing Enter pins the highlight (touch has no hover). A status line under the skills table spells the connection out in words.
